# Career questionnaire

Write your answers under each question. Short bullets and rough answers are fine; `/build-career-bank` turns them into proper wording. Write "skip" for anything that doesn't apply.

**The one rule:** only write what really happened. If you don't know a number, write "don't know" or give a rough estimate and mark it "approx." Nothing invented ever goes on your CV.

When you're done, run `/build-career-bank`.

---

## Part A: Your current CV
**A1.** What on your current CV do you **not** want to include any more?
>

**A2.** What is **missing** from it? (jobs, projects, courses, certificates, awards, societies)
>

**A3.** Dates for every item, as `MMM YYYY – MMM YYYY` (or "approx.").
>

**A4.** Contact details for CVs: phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub/portfolio.
>

## Part B: Education
**B1.** Each degree: grade/GPA, honours, dates.
>

**B2.** Thesis, capstone or biggest project: what was it, what did *you* do, which tools?
>

**B3.** 3–5 modules most relevant to the jobs you want (exact names, grades if good).
>

**B4.** Other courses and certificates (finished or in progress).
>

## Part C: Experience (the most important part)
**C1.** For each job, internship or volunteering role: title, what you actually did, tools, scale (people, money, data volume), results.
>

## Part D: Projects
**D1.** Every project worth mentioning (coursework, personal, hackathons, things you built with AI tools): problem, method, tools, outcome, link.
>

## Part E: Skills (interviews will test these)
**E1.** Rate each 0–3 and say how you'd prove it: Python, SQL, Excel, a BI tool, statistics, ML, others.
>

**E2.** Skills you never want listed, even if you've touched them.
>

**E3.** Languages and levels.
>

## Part F: Motivation and direction
**F1.** How did you get here, and why this field? The real story.
>

**F2.** Role types you want, ranked.
>

**F3.** Industries you're drawn to and any you'd avoid.
>

**F4.** What makes you different from other candidates with your degree?
>

## Part G: Stories
**G1.** A time you solved a problem with data or analysis.
>

**G2.** A team conflict or difficulty, and what you did.
>

**G3.** A time you led or organised people.
>

**G4.** A failure, and what you learned.
>

**G5.** Learning something new very fast.
>

**G6.** Persuading someone.
>

**G7.** Heavy pressure or a tight deadline.
>

**G8.** Something you did without being asked.
>

## Part H: Practical
**H1.** Work rights: status, allowed hours, exact wording you want on forms.
>

**H2.** Salary expectation, work mode, relocation, driving licence.
>

**H3.** Two referees (name, title, relation).
>

## Part I: Your voice
**I1.** Put old cover letters or personal statements into `me/writing_samples/`.

**I2.** How do you want to come across: formal, or direct and warm? Anything you hate in CVs or letters?
>
