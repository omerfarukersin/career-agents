# STAR stories

Writers and interview prep pick from these. Every story must be true as the user tells it; agents may tighten the wording but never add facts. `/build-career-bank` drafts them with you.

Template:

```markdown
### N. <Short title> (<theme>)
- Themes: (problem solving, teamwork, leadership, failure, pressure, persuasion, learning fast, adapting, initiative)
- Situation:
- Task:
- Action: (what *you* did)
- Result: (numbers if true; say "approx." when estimated)
- Lesson:
```
