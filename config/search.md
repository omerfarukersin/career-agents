# Search settings

Agents read this before searching, scoring or writing. The defaults below are a **country pack for Ireland** and a graduate search in data, analytics, AI and consulting. Edit everything to match your own search; see `docs/adapting.md` for another country.

Personal facts (your visa, your degree, your "never list" skills) belong in `me/`, not here.

## Target
- **Level:** graduate programmes, entry-level roles, internships / placements

- **Secondary:** internships and part-time roles, if your work rights allow (see `me/profile.md`). Score part-time roles 10 points lower than an equivalent graduate role.

## Role families (score family fit by tier)
- **Tier 1, strongest fit (15/15):** data / business analyst graduate; AI analyst / AI adoption / responsible AI; consulting analyst (management, technology, public sector)
- **Tier 2, strong fit (12/15):** junior data scientist; economics and policy analysis; risk / operations analytics
- **Tier 3, good fit (9/15):** operations / supply chain / commercial analyst; product and start-up roles
- **Tier 4 (5/15):** sales / business development
- **Avoid:** TODO (e.g. pure software engineering; tracks that require professional exams you don't want)
- **Graduate programmes open to any degree** hire for potential: give them a family floor of 12/15 when the rotations include analytical work.

## Location
- Country: **Ireland**
- Preferred: **Dublin** (incl. hybrid / remote-from-Dublin)
- Acceptable: Cork, Galway, Limerick, other Irish cities (score slightly lower)
- Exclude: roles requiring relocation outside the country

## Country conventions: Ireland (writers must follow)
- **CV:** 2 pages max, 1 page preferred for graduates (big tech: 1 page). No photo, no date of birth, no marital status.
- **Spelling:** Irish/UK (analyse, organisation, programme).
- **Cover letter:** 250–400 words, one page. Address a named person if found. Sign off "Kind regards".
- **Work rights:** include an eligibility line only with the exact wording in `me/profile.md`.
- **Grades:** Irish employers understand 1.1 / 2.1 / 2.2; convert or explain a foreign grade if useful.
- **Local business press for company research:** The Irish Times, Business Post, Silicon Republic, RTÉ Business.

## Timing notes (Ireland)
- Big graduate programmes (Big 4, banks, consultancies, multinationals) open **Sept–Nov** for the following autumn intake; many close Nov–Jan, and some close early when they fill. Apply to top fits in the first week a posting is live.
- Summer internships usually open Oct–Feb.
- Big tech graduate and intern roles in Dublin often open later (Oct–Jan).

## Eligibility rules for the analyst
- Score visa/eligibility against the work-rights section of `me/profile.md`.
- Flag, don't auto-reject: "no sponsorship" wording (some permits need none), "permanent right to work", citizenship lists, security clearance.
- **Graduation-date windows:** banks often require graduation inside a window (e.g. "must graduate between <month> and <month> <year>"). Check it before scoring.
- **Irish public bodies** (councils, state agencies, civil service) use a standard citizenship clause: read the Candidate Information Booklet first. They form panels and may make offers within months, so check the start date against your work rights. Foreign degrees usually need a **QQI recognition statement**.

Tailoring threshold: **60** (below → ask before spending effort). Recommend skipping outright only for real blockers (eligibility, a required degree you don't hold, a start date your work rights don't allow), not for fit alone.
