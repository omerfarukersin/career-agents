# Graduate Data Analyst, Harbourline Energy

## Original posting
Harbourline Energy supplies electricity and gas to 400,000 homes and businesses across Ireland and is investing in smart-meter data to help customers cut their energy use.

**The role**
Join our Customer Insights team for a two-year graduate programme. You will:
- Build and maintain reports and dashboards on customer usage, billing and service performance
- Write SQL to extract and join data from our customer data warehouse
- Use Python to automate recurring reports and analyses
- Analyse smart-meter data to find patterns in household demand
- Present findings to stakeholders in operations, pricing and marketing
- Help improve data quality across our customer systems

**About you**
- A 2.1 or higher degree (or expected) in economics, statistics, mathematics, engineering, computer science or a related discipline
- Working knowledge of SQL and Python
- Experience with a data visualisation tool such as Power BI or Tableau
- A clear communicator who can explain analysis to non-technical colleagues
- Curious, careful with detail, and keen to learn

**Nice to have**
- Interest in energy markets or sustainability
- Experience with forecasting or time-series analysis
- Familiarity with Git

Applications: CV and cover letter through our careers site by 15 February 2027. Shortlisted candidates complete an online numerical test and a video interview, followed by an assessment day. Candidates must be eligible to work in Ireland.
