\textbf{Re: Graduate Data Analyst (Ref: HL-GDA-27)}

Dear Hiring Team,

Your posting says Harbourline Energy is investing in smart-meter data to help customers cut their energy use. That line is why I am applying for the Graduate Data Analyst programme. My economics degree included a module in Energy and Environmental Economics, and my MSc thesis is on demand forecasting, using weekly point-of-sale data from retail rather than meter readings. I would like to bring that work to your Customer Insights team.

Most of the role is reporting, which is what I did as a data intern at CityWorks Facilities. Their weekly response-time report took about half a day to build by hand. I rebuilt it as SQL queries and a Python script, and checked the output against three past reports before switching, so the operations manager saw exactly the tables they were used to. It ran in a few minutes and went into the weekly meeting unchanged. I also built the Tableau dashboard of response times for 12 sites that the same meeting used.

You also ask for someone careful with detail. Before the figures went into that dashboard, I noticed that response times at two sites looked far too good. The cause was the logging form, which was creating duplicate tickets. I wrote a rule to flag them, so the dashboard showed corrected figures, and passed the form problem to IT. It taught me to check any number that looks too good.

Economics is the other half of what I offer. For my final-year project, graded 1.1, I estimated how public-transport demand responded to a fare cut, using OLS regression on two years of monthly data, and wrote up the result and its limits for readers without a statistics background. Your analysts present to pricing as well as operations, and that project was a pricing question answered in plain terms. As treasurer of the Economics Society I also managed a budget of about \texteuro6,000 and reported spending to the committee every month.

I finish my MSc in August 2027 and am available from September 2027 (Irish citizen; no sponsorship required). Thank you for reading my application. I would be glad to talk it through.

Kind regards,\\
Alex Quinn
