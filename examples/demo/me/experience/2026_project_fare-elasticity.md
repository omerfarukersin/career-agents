# Final-year project: public-transport fare elasticity, BA Economics, Shannon University
- Type: academic project
- Dates: Sept 2025 – Apr 2026
- Tools / methods: Python (pandas, statsmodels), OLS regression, Git

## What I did
- Combined two years of monthly ridership data from open data with fare and fuel-price series.
- Estimated price elasticity of demand with OLS, controlling for season and fuel prices.
- Wrote up the method and limits (short series, one fare change) for a non-technical reader.

## Results / numbers
- Estimated elasticity of about -0.3 (inelastic demand); project graded 1.1.

## What this proves
- Regression, careful handling of messy public data, explaining results simply.
