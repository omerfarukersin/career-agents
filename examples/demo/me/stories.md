# STAR stories

Fictional demo candidate.

## Career story
- Liked econometrics more than theory; the internship showed that most of the value was in getting clean data in front of the people making decisions, so Alex moved into analytics.

### 1. Automating the weekly report (problem solving with data)
- Situation: at CityWorks, the weekly response-time report took about half a day to build by hand.
- Task: make it faster without changing what the operations manager saw.
- Action: rewrote it as SQL queries plus a Python script; checked the output against three past reports before switching.
- Result: the report ran in a few minutes (approx.) and was used unchanged in the weekly meeting.
- Lesson: match the old output exactly first, then improve it.

### 2. Duplicate tickets (attention to detail)
- Situation: response times for two sites looked far too good.
- Task: find out why before the numbers went into the dashboard.
- Action: traced it to the logging form creating duplicate tickets; wrote a rule to flag them and a note for the team.
- Result: the dashboard showed corrected figures; the form issue was passed to IT.
- Lesson: a number that looks too good deserves a check.

### 3. Sponsorship for the conference (persuasion)
- Situation: the Economics Society's conference needed outside funding.
- Task: as treasurer, close the gap.
- Action: pitched to five local firms with a one-page plan of who would attend.
- Result: two sponsors signed; the year closed within budget.
- Lesson: make it easy for the other side to say yes.
